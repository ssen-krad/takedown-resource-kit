Step-by-Step Takedown Guides

Select a category below to find direct reporting links and pre-written templates.

🤖 Note on AI / Deepfakes: Yes, these guides work for AI-generated images too. Under both platform policies and Indian law (IT Rules 2021), "Synthetic Media" or "AI-generated nudity" is treated the same as real images. You have the same right to request a takedown within 24 hours.
Social Media Platforms

Facebook & Instagram (Meta)

Direct Link: Meta's NCII Reporting Form

Template to use:

I am reporting an image/video that violates Meta's policy on Non-Consensual Intimate Imagery. This content was shared without my consent and is causing me significant distress. The person in the image is me. Please remove it immediately as per your Community Standards.

X (formerly Twitter)

Direct Link: X's Private Information Form

Template to use:

I am reporting a violation of the Private Information and Media policy. This post contains intimate media of me, shared without my consent. This falls under your 'Non-consensual Nudity' rules. Please take immediate action.

TikTok

Direct Link: TikTok's Privacy Report Form

Template to use:

I am reporting content that violates the 'Nudity and Sexual Activities' and 'Harassment' policies. It features me in an intimate context without my consent. Please remove this content and review the account that posted it.

Reddit

Direct Link: Reddit's Report Form (Select "It's non-consensual intimate media").

Template to use:

This content is being reported for violating Reddit's sitewide policy against 'Non-Consensual Intimate Media'. I am the person depicted and I did not consent to this being shared. Please remove it urgently.

Telegram & Discord

For private chats, reporting the user is the best option. For public channels or servers, you can report the specific content.

Telegram: Report abusive users or public channels directly within the app by tapping on the profile/channel name and finding the 'Report' option.

Discord: Report any content to Discord's Trust & Safety team. Be as detailed as possible and provide message links if you can.

Adult Websites

Pornhub, XVideos, etc.

Most major adult sites have a "Content Removal" or "DMCA" link in the footer of their website. Look for these links first.

Template to use (focus on both NCII and Copyright):

I am formally requesting the immediate removal of this content. I am the person depicted, and this content was published without my consent, which violates your platform's terms of service. Furthermore, as the subject, I am asserting my rights related to this content. Please remove it urgently.
Search Engines

Google Search

Important: This only removes the link from search results, not from the original website. You must report it to the website first.

Direct Link: Google's Personal Information Removal Tool

Follow the on-screen instructions. They have a specific category for "doxxing and explicit personal images."

Microsoft Bing

Bing also has a dedicated form for reporting non-consensual imagery found in its search results.

Direct Link: Report a Concern to Bing

Select the option "A nonconsensual intimate image of me or someone else appears in Bing search results" and follow the steps.

DuckDuckGo, Yahoo, etc.

Most other search engines (like DuckDuckGo and Yahoo) are powered by Bing's and Google's search indexes. By successfully removing the content from Bing and Google, it will typically be removed from these other search engines automatically over time.

Direct Contact & Legal Notices

Notice to Indian Grievance Officer (IT Rules 2021)

Under Rule 3(2)(b) of IT Rules 2021, platforms must remove imagery showing partial/full nudity or sexual acts within 24 hours of a complaint. Find the "Grievance Officer" email in the platform's policy pages.

Template:

Subject: URGENT: Notice under Rule 3(2)(b) of Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021

To the Grievance Officer,

I am writing to notify you that content depicting me in a state of partial/full nudity or a sexual act, without my consent, is currently hosted on your platform. 
Under Rule 3(2)(b) of the IT Rules 2021, you are legally obligated to take all reasonable and practicable measures to remove or disable access to such content within 24 hours of receiving this complaint.

URL(s) of the content: [Insert URLs]
My profile/contact: [Insert your details]

Failure to remove this content promptly may result in loss of your intermediary safe harbor protection under Section 79 of the IT Act, 2000, and leave you liable for prosecution.

Awaiting your urgent action.

Direct Request to a Website Owner / Webmaster

Use this if a smaller website or forum is hosting the content and does not have a formal reporting form.

Template:

Subject: URGENT: Takedown Request - Privacy Violation and Non-Consensual Imagery

To the Webmaster/Owner of [Website Name],

I am writing to demand the immediate removal of content hosted on your website at the following URL(s): [Insert URLs]

This content constitutes non-consensual intimate imagery (NCII) and features me without my authorization. Its continued publication is a severe violation of my privacy rights, and may be a strict liability offense under local and international digital laws. 

I demand that you immediately disable access to and permanently delete this content within 24 hours. Failure to do so will result in me escalating this matter to law enforcement and seeking legal remedies against your hosting service.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]

Cease and Desist to Known Perpetrator

If you know who is distributing the content and have a way to message them securely, this strict warning may stop them. (Do not use this if they are violent or if law enforcement advised you against contact).

Template:

This is a formal Cease and Desist notice. 

You are currently in possession of, and actively distributing, intimate photos/videos of me without my consent. This is a severe criminal offense under the Information Technology Act (Sections 66E, 67, 67A) and the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita.

You must immediately:
1. Delete all copies of this material from all your devices.
2. Stop sending or threatening to send this material to anyone.
3. Remove it from any platform where you have already uploaded it.

I have already preserved all evidence of your threats and uploads, including timestamps and screenshots. If you do not comply immediately, I will hand this evidence directly to the Cyber Crime Police and pursue maximum legal action against you, which can include imprisonment and heavy fines.

Do not contact me again except to confirm that all materials have been permanently destroyed.
Global Proactive Tools

StopNCII.org

This is a critical proactive tool. It stops images from being spread on partner platforms *before* they appear.

How it works: You select the images/videos on your own device. The tool creates a unique hash (a digital fingerprint) without uploading your actual file. This hash is shared with partner companies (like Meta, TikTok, etc.) so they can block any future attempts to upload that content.

Direct Link: Visit StopNCII.org
