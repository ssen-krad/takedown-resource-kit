Regional & Country-Specific Helplines

USA

The Cyber Civil Rights Initiative (CCRI) runs a 24/7 crisis helpline for residents.

CCRI Image Abuse Helpline: 1-844-878-2274

CCRI Website Report to FBI (IC3)

Brazil

Brazil has specific laws against online image abuse. Report directly to the Federal Police or a local women's support organization.

Virtual Police Station

United Kingdom

The Revenge Porn Helpline is the primary support service, offering confidential advice and takedown help.

Phone: 0345 6000 459

Report Harmful Content

Europe (General)

Every EU country has a Safer Internet Centre. You also have rights under GDPR, including the "Right to be Forgotten."

Find Your EU Centre

Australia

The eSafety Commissioner is a government agency with legal power to compel takedowns.

Report to eSafety

Philippines

The PNP Anti-Cybercrime Group is the primary agency. The Philippines also has strong laws against Violence Against Women and Children (VAWC).

Contact PNP

South Africa

Report online harassment to the South African Police Service (SAPS). Gender-based violence NGOs like POWA may also offer support.

Report to SAPS Contact POWA

For All Other Countries 🌏

If your country is not listed, start with these steps:

  • Search online for "[Your Country Name] cyber crime police".
  • Contact your national or local police service.
  • Reach out to national women's rights or anti-violence NGOs.
  • Always use the platform's own reporting tools first.
