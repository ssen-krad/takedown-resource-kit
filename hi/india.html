Reporting and Support in India

Find official government portals and state-wise police contact information.

National Level Helplines

National Cyber Crime Portal

The official government portal to report all types of cybercrime. You can file a complaint anonymously.

File a Complaint

Women's Helpline

For immediate assistance, dial the pan-India number for the Women's Helpline.

Dial: 1091 / 181

Men's Helpline & Sextortion

Men facing financial sextortion or deepfake laundering threats should report immediately. Do not pay. Consult a cyber lawyer.

Cyber Police: 1930

Emergency Response

For any emergency situation that requires immediate police assistance.

Dial: 112
